📚 IT Knowledge Base

Find solutions to common IT problems

📶 Wi-Fi Not Working

Check whether Wi-Fi is enabled. Restart the Wi-Fi adapter and reconnect to the college network.

🔑 Forgot Password

Use the password reset option or contact the IT support team to reset your account.

🖨️ Printer Not Working

Check the printer connection, paper, power and printer queue.

🌐 College Portal Not Opening

Check your internet connection, clear browser cache and try opening the portal again.

💻 Computer Running Slowly

Close unnecessary applications, restart the computer and check available storage.

📧 Email Problem

Check your internet connection and login credentials. Contact IT support if the problem continues.
